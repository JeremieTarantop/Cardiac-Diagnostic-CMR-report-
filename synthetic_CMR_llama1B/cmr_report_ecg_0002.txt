Clinical Indications: The patient presented for evaluation of chest pain and dyspnea. They were found to have an abnormal ECG that suggested left ventricular hypertrophy or systolic heart failure. This finding is consistent with the presence of pericardial effusion.

Findings:
- Sinus bradycardia (SCP code NORM): A normal sinus rhythm was observed at rest. However, during exercise, there was a significant decrease in the rate from 120 beats/min to 80 beats/min. This suggests left ventricular hypertrophy or systolic heart failure.

Conclusion:
Based on this ECG, we can conclude that the patient has a clinically significant diagnosis of left ventricular hypertrophy or systolic heart failure. We will need further testing to confirm this diagnosis. It is recommended that they undergo a full workup including echocardiography, coronary angiogram, and stress test.
