In anterior leads such as V1-V4, typical ischemic changes or infarction markers are less apparent, suggesting an isolated inferior myocardial event. Collectively, the evidence gathered from the inferior lead abnormalities, when correlated with the clinical context, supports the diagnosis of an inferior myocardial infarct of an undetermined age, as indicated by the combination of altered depolarization and repolarization markers, notwithstanding the sinus rhythm presenting as part of a generally abnormal ECG.
