The QT intervals are within acceptable ranges, although there is some variability in calculated QTc, but such findings are expected in paced rhythms.

Additionally, absence of intrinsic QRS complexes in leads such as II, III, and aVF supports the dependence on ventricular pacing. Therefore, the only notable findings on this ECG are consistent with normal function of a dual-chamber pacemaker, confirming an abnormal ECG classification due only to pacing.
