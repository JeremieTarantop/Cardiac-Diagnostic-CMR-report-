The PR intervals across different leads vary but are generally within normal limits, ruling out any significant atrioventricular conduction delay.

The QRS duration is within the upper limit of normal; however, there is poor R wave progression noted from leads V1 to V4. This can sometimes be a normal variant but should be correlated clinically to exclude anterior myocardial infarction or anterior ischemia, particularly if it's a new finding.

Moreover, the T wave morphology appears normal and consistent across leads without inversion or hyperacute changes, further supporting the absence of acute ischemia.

The computed QT and QTc intervals are slightly prolonged in some leads (especially in Leads I, II, III, V2, V3, V4, V5), bringing into consideration the risk for arrhythmias, though this could also be rate-related due to tachycardia.
