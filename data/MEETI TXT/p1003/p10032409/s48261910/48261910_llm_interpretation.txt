Lead aVR, however, is unremarkable except for slightly elevated P and T wave amplitudes, which are not diagnostic in isolation. In the absence of acute changes, the combination of these findings aligns with a junctional rhythm and an age-indeterminate anteroseptal infarction diagnosis. Given the chronic nature suggested by stable changes, acute intervention may not be immediately necessary, but the patient should be monitored for chronic heart disease management.
