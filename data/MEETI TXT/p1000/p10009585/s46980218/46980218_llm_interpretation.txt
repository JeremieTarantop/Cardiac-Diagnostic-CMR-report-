This can often be seen in mild ischemic changes, which can be difficult to definitively characterize as ischemic versus non-ischemic (such as changes due to myocardial demand) without clinical correlation.

This necessitates a thorough clinical assessment to correlate these findings with the patient's clinical picture, considering the broad spectrum of differentials that could cause such an ECG appearance.
